Whether an observer slot ([RadialObserverSlot](../radial-observer-slot/)) orbits or keeps a fixed angle.

<!-- members -->

### Orbit

An orbit slot. It orbits automatically, rotates with a right-button drag inside the slot, and zooms with the wheel. After input stops for a set time it resumes orbiting ([RadialObserverOrbit](../radial-observer-orbit/)).

### Fixed

A fixed slot. It looks from the angle given by the slot's `Yaw` and `Pitch`.
